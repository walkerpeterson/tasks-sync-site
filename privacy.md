# Tasks Sync - Privacy Policy

Tasks Sync accesses Google Tasks data (task lists, titles, notes, due dates and completion status) only to copy tasks between the account owner's own Google accounts.

The data is processed on the owner's own computer, is not stored on any server, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
